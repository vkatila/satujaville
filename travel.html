Extra days in Lapland (and a little Oulu)

If you have a chance to stay a bit longer in the most beautiful and peaceful region of the world (yes we said it!), here are some recommendations. We also include ideas for Oulu since it is a European Capital of Culture in 2026 and relatively close to the wedding venue (at least from a Lappish perspective).

Quick heads-up: opening hours and seasonal availability can change. Please double-check official pages before you go.
Rovaniemi Oulu Practical tips

Rovaniemi

Rovaniemi is the Official Home of Santa Claus, but also the capital of Finnish Lapland. Due to overwhelming tourism in the winter season, it offers very good conditions for a summer holiday as well — especially for more affordable accommodation and a calmer atmosphere.

Christmas & Santa

If you are a fan of Christmas, there are several locations with that theme. Main attractions (also available in summertime) include:

  • Santa Claus Village (Arctic Circle line, shops, cafés, Santa’s Office, postcards)
  • SantaPark (indoor Christmas theme park)
  • Santa Claus Main Post Office (send a card with an Arctic Circle postmark)

Nature

Rovaniemi is the largest city in Europe measured by land area. The surrounding nature is peaceful and beautiful, even though this region is not yet “tunturi” country (tunturi = a rounded, treeless fell).

  • Easy trails near town (forest walks, riverside routes)
  • Lakes and rivers (Kemijoki & Ounasjoki area)
  • Viewpoints (sunset / midnight sun season walks)
  • Laavut (public campfire shelters) — perfect for a break outdoors
  • Fishing (check local permits and rules)

Museums & indoor things

  • Arktikum (museum & Arctic science centre)
  • Pilke Science Centre (forests, northern nature, family-friendly)

Accommodation ideas

Summer is often easier for booking and prices. Here are a few starting points:

  • Santa Claus Holiday Village (cabins right by Santa Claus Village)
  • Arctic TreeHouse Hotel (memorable “splurge” stay)
  • Hostel Café Koti (good budget option)
  • Scandic Rovaniemi City (central hotel)

Oulu

Oulu is a coastal city with great summer vibes: beaches, islands, cycling paths and long sunsets by the sea. It’s also a European Capital of Culture in 2026 — a fun add-on if you have extra days.

What to experience in Oulu?

  • Nallikari Beach (classic summer spot)
  • Walking & cycling around the waterfront and islands (easy and very “Oulu”)
  • City centre stroll: market area + cafés (great for a relaxed afternoon)
  • Oulu2026 programme browsing (events & culture)

Accommodation ideas

  • Nallikari Holiday Village (stay by the beach)
  • Radisson Blu Hotel, Oulu (central, easy base)
  • Lapland Hotels Oulu (central)

Practical tips

  • Distances: things can look “close” on a map but still take time.
  • Layers: summer evenings can be cool — especially near water.
  • Mosquito plan: bring repellent for lakeside/forest evenings.
  • Laavu etiquette: shared spaces — leave it clean for the next person.
  • Check schedules: opening hours may change by season and weekday.
