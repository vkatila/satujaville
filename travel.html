Rovaniemi

Rovaniemi is the Official Home of Santa Claus, but also the capital of Finnish Lapland. Due to overwhelming tourism in the winter season, it offers very good conditions for a summer holiday as well in regards of affordable accommodation and variety of culinary treats.

Practical tips

Good to know Lapland-friendly

Small things that help a lot

  • Distances: things can look “close” on a map but still take time.
  • Layers: summer evenings can be cool, especially near water.
  • Mosquito plan: bring repellent for lakeside/forest evenings.
  • Laavu etiquette: shared spaces — leave it clean for the next person.
  • Always check hours: seasonal schedules change.
